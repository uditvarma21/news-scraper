Coastal Security

Exercise Sea Vigil 2026

16-11-2026

Source: PIB 

The Indian Navy conducted the 5th edition of the biennial nationwide coastal defence exercise ‘Sea Vigil 2026’ covering India's entire coastline and Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) to assess maritime security preparedness and inter-agency coordination. 

  • Background & Origin: The Sea Vigil exercise was conceptualized in 2018 to test the coastal security measures implemented after the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks. 
  • Strategic Importance: 'Sea Vigil' acts as a crucial precursor and build-up to the Indian Navy’s major biennial Theatre Level Readiness Operational Exercise (TROPEX), which evaluates broader naval preparedness, including high-seas operations. 
  • Multi-Agency Synergy: It is led by the Indian Navy and features a whole-of-government approach, integrating stakeholders like the Indian Coast Guard, Coastal Security Police, Customs, Intelligence Bureau (IB), and the Central Industrial Security Force (CISF). 
  • Primary Objective: Activate the entire coastal defence mechanism across the nation, test technical information-sharing, and plug existing security gaps to secure India's maritime borders. 
  • Two-Phased Execution: 
    • Phase 1 - CDSRE (Coastal Defence and Security Readiness Evaluation): Involves Joint Audit Teams inspecting the security and operational preparedness of critical maritime infrastructure like major/minor ports, fish landing centres, and Vulnerable Areas and Vulnerable Points (VAs/VPs). 
    • Phase 2 - Tactical Phase: Involves live deployment of maritime assets dividing forces into a simulated 'Red Force' (attacking) and 'Blue Force' (defending) to evaluate real-time response mechanisms. 
  • Contemporary Threat Mitigation: The exercise tests readiness against modern asymmetric threats, including drone attacks, merchant vessel hijackings, and covert strikes on vital coastal installations.
